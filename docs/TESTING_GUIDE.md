Follow [README login instructions](../README.md#email-otp-login-website-and-android) and [the SMTP migration guide](../deploy/RENDER.md). ## AR: getting the marks to appear

Use an ARCore-supported physical Android phone with Google Play Services for AR installed and camera permission allowed. Practice mode has buttons and intentionally has no camera tracking or placement ring.

1. Sign in, open **Training**, select a module and choose **Start AR training**.
2. Point the camera down at an unobstructed, well-lit, textured floor. Slowly move the phone sideways so AR can recognize the surface. Plain shiny floors and low light can prevent detection.
3. The center ring is **red while scanning** and **green when the center points at a detected floor**. A red ring is not a detected hazard.
4. When green, tap **Place equipment**, or tap a detected floor in the camera view. Virtual equipment and labels now appear. Nothing appears automatically merely from opening the camera.
5. Keep roughly **0.4–4 metres horizontally from the placement point**, aim back at it and follow the current instruction. Red extinguisher/hazard equipment and a green exit are virtual training objects, not automatic identification of real equipment.
6. Tap the labelled target requested by the instruction. For the **Sweep** target, drag sideways and finish on its label. A wrong target does not advance the step.
7. If equipment is off-screen or tracking is lost, look back at the training area or use **Reposition**, scan until green, then **Place equipment** again. Reposition moves the scene; it does not reset lesson progress.
8. Finish the sequence and tap **Save training** after at least 30 seconds. When online it synchronizes; offline it queues for later. Complete the assessment and an independent practical review for certification.

The AR renderer does not recognize real fires, gas, machines, hazards or safe zones. Test in a clear training space without live hazards. If your device is unsupported, use the explicitly labelled practice mode. Guest practice does not save a qualification to a worker account.

## Detailed AR equipment and replayable demo

Every training session opens an interactive tutorial. Tap Next after the scan animation, tap the green sign in the demo, then drag sideways across the gesture panel. Tutorial actions and tutorial duration do not count toward training completion. Close it to return to the lesson and reopen it anytime using the **Demo** button in the top bar; current lesson progress remains intact.

The renderer now generates detailed equipment with smooth 64-segment bodies, metal highlights, hose/valve/handle/pin assemblies, labelled alarm panels, PPE, detector displays, clipboards, framed exit/stop/hazard signs, and 1024-square vector textures with mipmaps. Detector dashes indicate a simulation, not live readings. These signs are original training illustrations, not certified safety signage.

The GLB files in the old models directory are placeholders and are not loaded. The new geometry and artwork are generated by EquipmentRenderer. A debug-only EquipmentPreviewActivity provides a camera-free GPU visual check; it is absent from release builds. Physical AR tracking, lighting and performance still require device testing.

## Placement distance, fullscreen and the next action

Aim at a textured floor **1.8–4 metres ahead**. A green ring means a tracked floor is available at that distance; tap **Place equipment**. **Auto-place • 2.5 m** is an alternative when holding the phone upright; it uses an estimated height rather than detecting the floor. Objects face the worker and the remaining rows extend away from them. Stay 1.2–5 metres from the placement point during interaction.

Tap **Fullscreen** in the top bar to hide system bars and reduce the instruction panels. The current step and Demo stay accessible. Scroll the compact instruction panel for voice guidance, feedback, Reposition, or Save training. Tap **Exit fullscreen** to restore the expanded view without resetting progress.

After placement, follow the current step. Its target label is highlighted green; tap the label above the equipment, rather than the placement ring. In Fire & Explosion Response, the sequence is **Exit → Alarm → Extinguisher → Pin → Base → Handle → Sweep → Exit**. For Sweep, drag sideways and release on its label. Correct actions advance the step; wrong targets show feedback. When the sequence is complete, tap **Save training** (at least 30 seconds of training is required). Assessment and practical review remain separate.

On a physical phone, verify that a floor hit closer than 1.8 m stays red, auto-placement is ahead in different camera directions, highlighted labels advance with the steps, and toggling fullscreen keeps both the anchor and current step intact. Reposition should return to scanning without resetting lesson progress.

## Animated pin and fire effects

At the Pull step, tap the green Pin label. Watch the pin shaft and ring withdraw from the extinguisher valve and lift clear over 1.4 seconds. The lesson advances after the animation; repeated taps during the movement are ignored. Completed pin removal is restored with saved lesson progress. Practice mode retains its button-based interaction.

The fire lesson uses camera-facing flame tongues with soft transparent edges, yellow cores, orange tips and turbulent rising smoke. Animation uses elapsed time rather than a fixed frame increment. Fire appears at Base, not at the extinguisher or in unrelated lessons. On a physical phone, check the effect at different viewing angles and verify that solid equipment occludes it correctly. The debug EquipmentPreviewActivity accepts --ez effects true for a looping pin/fire rendering preview without AR tracking.
