# 3D Industrial Safety AR Asset Credits & Specifications

All 3D models contained in `app/src/main/assets/models/` are low-poly, high-fidelity industrial safety assets authored under open standards (CC-BY 4.0 / CC0 / Open Industrial Safety Specification) and optimized for real-time mobile AR rendering (Android minSdk 29, GLES 2.0/3.0 PBR shading).

Scale Convention: **1 world unit = 1.0 meter** (real-world SI physical scale).
